Leadership

Built by people who played.

The Tennis Cricket Association Maharashtra (TCAM) is affiliated to the Tennis Cricket Association of India (TCAI), the official governing body for tennis cricket in Maharashtra. Our mission: nurture and promote tennis cricket at the grassroots level, identify and develop young talent across all districts, and provide a pathway to state and national representation.

  • Promote tennis cricket across all 36 districts of Maharashtra
  • Organize structured district and state-level tournaments
  • Develop coaching programs in partnership with local academies
  • Establish strong district-level associations
  • Identify and nurture talent for national representation
